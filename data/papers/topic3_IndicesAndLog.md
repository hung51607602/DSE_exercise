### 3A.1 HKCEE MA 1987(A) – I – 3(a)

**題目**

Simplify $\sqrt{\dfrac{3^{5k+2}}{27^k}}$.

**答案**

$\sqrt{\dfrac{3^{5k+2}}{27^k}} = \left(\dfrac{3^{5k+2}}{3^{3k}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = (3^{2k+2})^{\frac{1}{2}} = 3^{k+1}$

## 3B Logarithms（對數）

---

### 3B.1 HKCEE MA 1986(A) – I – 5(a)

**題目**

Evaluate $\log_2 8 + \log_2 \dfrac{1}{16}$.

**答案**

$\log_2 8 + \log_2 \dfrac{1}{16} = \log_2 2^3 + \log_2 2^{-4} = 3 + (-4) = -1$

---

### 3B.2 HKCEE MA 1987(A) – I – 3(b)

**題目**

Simplify $\dfrac{\log a^3b^2 - \log ab^2}{\log\sqrt{a}}$.

**答案**

$\dfrac{\log a^3b^2 - \log ab^2}{\log\sqrt{a}} = \dfrac{\log\dfrac{a^3b^2}{ab^2}}{\frac{1}{2}\log a} = \dfrac{\log a^2}{\frac{1}{2}\log a} = \dfrac{2\log a}{\frac{1}{2}\log a} = 4$

---

### 3B.3 HKCEE MA 1988 – I – 6

**題目**

Given that $\log 2 = r$ and $\log 3 = s$, express the following in terms of $r$ and $s$:

(a) $\log 18$,
(b) $\log 15$.

**答案**

(a) $\log 18 = \log 2\cdot 3^2 = \log 2 + 2\log 3 = r + 2s$

(b) $\log 15 = \log\dfrac{3\times 10}{2} = \log 3 + 1 - \log 2 = s + 1 - r$

---

### 3B.4 HKCEE MA 1990 – I – 2(b)

**題目**

Simplify $\dfrac{\log(a^2) + \log(b^4)}{\log(ab^2)}$, where $a, b > 0$.

**答案**

$\dfrac{\log(a^2) + \log(b^4)}{\log(ab^2)} = \dfrac{\log a^2b^4}{\log ab^2} = \dfrac{\log(ab^2)^2}{\log ab^2} = \dfrac{2\log ab^2}{\log ab^2} = 2$

Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the roots of the equation $10x^2 + 20x + 1 = 0$. Without solving the equation, find the values of

(a) $4^\alpha \times 4^\beta$,

**答案**

$\begin{cases}\alpha + \beta = -2\\ \alpha\beta = \dfrac{1}{10}\end{cases}$

(a) $4^\alpha \times 4^\beta = 4^{\alpha+\beta} = 4^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{16}$

(b) $\log_{10}\alpha + \log_{10}\beta = \log_{10}\alpha\beta = \log_{10}\dfrac{1}{10} = -1$

### 3B.9 HKDSE MA SP – I – 17

**題目**

A researcher defined Scale $A$ and Scale $B$ to represent the magnitude of an explosion as shown in the table:

| Scale | Formula |
|---|---|
| A | $M = \log_4 E$ |
| B | $N = \log_8 E$ |

It is given that $M$ and $N$ are the magnitudes of an explosion on Scale $A$ and Scale $B$ respectively, while $E$ is the relative energy released by the explosion. If the magnitude of an explosion is 6.4 on Scale $B$, find the magnitude of the explosion on Scale $A$.

**答案**

*Method 1*

$6.4 = \log_8 E \Rightarrow E = 8^{6.4}$

$\therefore M = \log_4 E = \log_4(8^{6.4}) = \dfrac{\log_2 8^{6.4}}{\log_2 4} = \dfrac{\log_2 2^{3(6.4)}}{\log_2 2^2} = \dfrac{19.2}{2} = 9.6$

*Method 2*

$\begin{cases}M = \log_4 E\\ N = \log_8 E\end{cases} \Rightarrow \begin{cases}E = 4^M\\ E = 8^N\end{cases} \Rightarrow 4^M = 8^N$

$2^{2M} = 2^{3N}$

$M = \dfrac{3}{2}N = \dfrac{3}{2}(6.4) = 9.6$

### 3B.10 HKDSE MA 2014 – I – 15

**題目**

The graph in the figure shows the linear relation between $\log_4 x$ and $\log_8 y$. The slope and the intercept on the horizontal axis of the graph are $\dfrac{-1}{3}$ and 3 respectively. Express the relation between $x$ and $y$ in the form $y = Ax^k$, where $A$ and $k$ are constants.

**圖片描述：** 一個直角坐標圖。橫軸標示為 $\log_4 x$，縱軸標示為 $\log_8 y$。原點標示為 $O$。圖中有一條由左上方向右下方傾斜的直線（斜率為負），該直線與橫軸相交於橫軸上標示為 3 的位置。

**答案**

*Method 1*

From the graph, $(\log_4 x, \log_8 y) = (3, 0)$ and Slope $= \dfrac{-1}{3}$.

Using point-slope form, the equation is:

$\log_8 y - 0 = \dfrac{-1}{3}(\log_4 x - 3)$

$\log_8 y = \dfrac{-1}{3}\log_4 x + 1$

$= \log_4\left(x^{\frac{-1}{3}}\cdot 4\right)$

$\dfrac{\log_2 y}{\log_2 8} = \dfrac{\log_2 4x^{\frac{-1}{3}}}{\log_2 4}$

$\dfrac{\log_2 y}{3} = \dfrac{\log_2 4x^{\frac{-1}{3}}}{2}$

$\log_2 y = \dfrac{3}{2}\log_2 4x^{\frac{-1}{3}}$

$= \log_2\left(4x^{\frac{-1}{3}}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = \log_2 8x^{\frac{-1}{2}}$

$\Rightarrow y = 8x^{\frac{-1}{2}}$

*Method 2*

$(\log_4 x, \log_8 y) = (3, 0) \Rightarrow (x, y) = (64, 1)$

Let the point of the line cutting the vertical axis be $(0, b)$.

$\dfrac{b - 0}{0 - 3} = \dfrac{-1}{3} \Rightarrow b = 1$

$\therefore (\log_4 x, \log_8 y) = (0, 1) \Rightarrow (x, y) = (1, 8)$

Putting into $y = Ax^k$,

$\begin{cases}8 = A\\ 1 = A(64)^k \Rightarrow 1 = 8^{1+2k} \Rightarrow k = \dfrac{-1}{2}\end{cases}$

Hence, $y = 8x^{\frac{-1}{2}}$.

*Method 3*

$y = Ax^k \Rightarrow \log_2 y = \log_2 Ax^k = \log_2 A + k\log_2 x$

$\dfrac{\log_8 y}{\log_8 2} = \log_8 A + k\dfrac{\log_4 x}{\log_4 2}$

$3\log_8 y = \log_2 A + 2k\log_4 x$

$\log_8 y = \dfrac{2k}{3}\log_4 x + \dfrac{1}{3}\log_2 A$

From theory of straight lines,

$\begin{cases}\dfrac{-1}{3} = \text{Slope} = \dfrac{2k}{3} \Rightarrow k = \dfrac{-1}{2}\\[2mm] 3 = x\text{-intercept} = -\dfrac{\frac{1}{3}\log_2 A}{\frac{2k}{3}} = \dfrac{-1}{2k}\log_2 A \Rightarrow A = 2^3 = 8\end{cases}$

Hence, $y = 8x^{\frac{-1}{2}}$.

### 3B.11 HKDSE MA 2017 – I – 15

**題目**

Let $a$ and $b$ be constants. Denote the graph of $y = a + \log_b x$ by $G$. The $x$-intercept of $G$ is 9 and $G$ passes through the point $(243, 3)$. Express $x$ in terms of $y$.

**答案**

$G$ passes through $(9, 0)$ and $(243, 3)$

$\Rightarrow \begin{cases}0 = a + \log_b 9\\ 3 = a + \log_b 243\end{cases} \Rightarrow 3 = \log_b 243 - \log_b 9 = \log_b\dfrac{243}{9}$

$\Rightarrow b^3 = 27 \Rightarrow b = 9 \Rightarrow a = -\log_b 9 = -2$

$\therefore y = -2 + \log_3 x \Rightarrow \log_3 x = y + 2 \Rightarrow x = 3^{y+2}$

## 3C Exponential and logarithmic equations（指數與對數方程）

---

### 3C.1 HKCEE MA 1980(3) – I – 7

**題目**

Find $x$ if $\log_3(x-3) + \log_3(x+3) = 3$.

**答案**

$\log_3(x-3) + \log_3(x+3) = 3$

$\log_3(x-3)(x+3) = 3$

$x^2 - 9 = 27$

$x = 6$ or $-6$ (rejected)

---

### 3C.2 HKCEE MA 1981(1) – I – 5 & 1981(2) – I – 6

**題目**

Solve $4^x = 10 - 4^{x+1}$.

**答案**

$4^x = 10 - 4^{x+1}$

$4^x = 10 - 4^x\cdot 4$

$(1+4)4^x = 10$

$4^x = 2 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

---

### 3C.3 HKCEE MA 1982(1/2) – I – 2

**題目**

If $\begin{cases}4^{x-y} = 4\\ 4^{x+y} = 16\end{cases}$, solve for $x$ and $y$.

**答案**

$\begin{cases}4^{x-y} = 4 \Rightarrow x - y = 1\\ 4^{x+y} = 16 \Rightarrow x + y = 2\end{cases} \Rightarrow \begin{cases}x = \dfrac{3}{2}\\ y = \dfrac{1}{2}\end{cases}$

---

### 3C.4 HKCEE MA 1985(B) – I – 3

**題目**

Solve $2^{2x} - 3(2^x) - 4 = 0$.

**答案**

$2^{2x} - 3(2^x) - 4 = 0$

$(2^x)^2 - 3(2^x) - 4 = 0$

$(2^x - 4)(2^x + 1) = 0$

$2^x = 4$ or $-1$ (rejected) $\Rightarrow x = 2$

---

### 3C.5 HKCEE MA 1986(A) – I – 5(b)

**題目**

If $2\log_{10}x - \log_{10}y = 0$, show that $y = x^2$.

**答案**

$2\log_{10}x - \log_{10}y = 0$

$\log_{10}x^2 = \log_{10}y$

$x^2 = y$

### 3C.6 HKCEE MA 1987(B) – I – 3

**題目**

Solve the equation $3^{2x} + 3^x - 2 = 0$.

**答案**

$3^{2x} + 3^x - 2 = 0$

$(3^x)^2 + (3^x) - 2 = 0$

$(3^x + 2)(3^x - 1) = 0$

$3^x = -2$ (rejected) or $1 \Rightarrow x = 0$

### 3C.8 HKCEE MA 1995 – I – 7

**題目**

Solve the following equations without using a calculator:

(a) $3^x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{27}}$;
(b) $\log x + 2\log 4 = \log 48$.

**答案**

(a) $3^x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{27}} = 27^{-\frac{1}{2}} = (3^3)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$

 $x = \dfrac{-3}{2}$

(b) $\log x + 2\log 4 = \log 48$

 $\log x + \log 4^2 = \log 48$

 $\log 16x = \log 48 \Rightarrow 16x = 48 \Rightarrow x = 3$
