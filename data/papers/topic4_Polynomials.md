### 4A.1 HKCEE MA 1980(1/1*/3) – I – 2

**題目**

Factorize
(a) $a(3b-c)+c-3b$,
(b) $x^4-1$.

**答案**

(a) $a(3b-c)+c-3b = (3b-c)(a-1)$

(b) $x^4-1 = (x-1)(x+1)(x^2+1)$

### 4A.2 HKCEE MA 1981(2/3) – I – 5

**題目**

Factorize $(1+x)^4-(1-x^2)^2$.

**答案**

$(1+x)^4-(1-x^2)^2 = [(1+x)^2]^2-(1-x^2)^2$
$= [(1+x)^2-(1-x^2)][(1+x)^2+(1-x^2)]$
$= (2x+2x^2)(2+2x) = 4x(1+x)^2$

---

### 4A.3 HKCEE MA 1983(A/B) – I – 1

**題目**

Factorise $(x^2+4x+4)-(y-1)^2$.

**答案**

$(x^2+4x+4)-(y-1)^2 = (x+2)^2-(y-1)^2$
$= [(x+2)-(y-1)][(x+2)+(y-1)]$
$= (x-y+3)(x+y+1)$

### 4A.4 HKCEE MA 1984(A/B) – I – 4

**題目**

Factorize
(a) $x^2y+2xy+y$,
(b) $x^2y+2xy+y-y^3$.

**答案**

(a) $x^2y+2xy+y = y(x^2+2x+1) = y(x+1)^2$

(b) $x^2y+2xy+y-y^3 = y(x+1)^2-y^3$
$= y[(x+1)^2-y^2]$
$= y(x+1-y)(x+1+y)$

### 4B.1 HKCEE MA 1980(1*/3) – I – 13(a)

**題目**

It is given that $f(x)=2x^2+ax+b$.
(i) If $f(x)$ is divided by $(x-1)$, the remainder is $-5$. If $f(x)$ is divided by $(x+2)$, the remainder is 4. Find the values of $a$ and $b$.
(ii) If $f(x)=0$, find the value of $x$.

**答案**

$\Rightarrow \begin{cases}a=-1\\ b=-6\end{cases}$

(ii) $f(x)=0$
$2x^2-x-6=0$
$(2x+3)(x-2)=0 \Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{3}{2}$ or $2$
